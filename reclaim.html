Reclaim

Reclaim brings together mixed media installations from 2020 and 2021. Obstructed Views was installed at Goldie Mill Ruins in Guelph, and Reclaim was installed at Westminster Ponds and Veterans Village in London, Ontario. This work was supported by the Ontario Arts Council.
